What this is

Nepali shops announce sales on Instagram, then bury them three clicks deep on their own site. SaleKhoj reads the price tags for you: once a day we check every store we track, keep the products whose price actually fell, and put them on one page.

What counts as a deal

A product only shows up here if the store publishes both a current price and a higher original price, and the gap lands between 5% and 90%. We never read a "SALE" badge and guess a number. Under 5% is noise; over 90% is almost always a data entry error. Both get dropped rather than shown.

Where the prices come from

Straight from each store's own product feed — the same data their website renders. We store the price, the original price, the image and the link, nothing else. We don't repackage their catalogue, we point at it. Prices can change between our check and your click; the store's page is always the truth.

How this makes money

Right now, it doesn't. Links are plain outbound links and we take nothing when you buy. Eventually some will become affiliate links, and we'll mark them when they are. We won't re-order the list to favour the ones that pay — the sort you choose is the sort you get.

Something wrong?

If you run one of these shops and want your products off the list, say so and they're gone the same day. If a price is stale or a link is dead, tell us which one — that's a bug, not a feature.
